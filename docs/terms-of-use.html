Sous is a hackathon prototype submitted to the Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon. It is provided as is, for demonstration and evaluation.

What Sous is for

Sous suggests recipes from ingredients you describe, lists ingredients you're missing, and records meals you ask it to schedule. It draws from a small, fixed recipe catalog.

Allergies and food safety

Allergy and dietary filtering is best effort. It matches ingredient names against a limited list of allergen families and can miss hidden or cross-contaminated allergens. Always check ingredient labels yourself. Do not rely on Sous as your only safeguard against an allergy or a medical dietary requirement.
